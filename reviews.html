"Tucked away in a tiny town, (known as Southport, CT.), this area is something out of a Steinbeck novel. Quaint, charming, and perfect in its own way. Besides cards, it's packed with ornaments, charming knick-knacks, paper weights, postcards, brick & brack, dish towels, and so many unique objects (too many to list). Smacked dab in the middle of this organized explosion of wonderfulness is Barbara. A whimsical character who is always upbeat, stylish and an incredible representation of what she sells.
